# Softstage Store trailer R2 storyboard

## Format

- Frame size: 1920 x 1080.
- Frame rate: 30 fps.
- Length: 933 frames, 31.1 seconds.
- Picture: real Explorer and Photos recordings, with time-compressed pauses.
- Sound: quiet transition effects. No voice or music.

The cut moves from the command close-up back to the first take after its command has run. The first take shows the matching command action and one PNG output tile. Its crop excludes the Explorer preview pane. The second take has two older output thumbnails outside the close-up crop. The close-up crop excludes them. The trailer edits multiple real recordings together and does not present them as one take. Exactly one generated composition appears at a time: first the Explorer tile, then the full Photos view.

Explorer framing uses a tight source-image crop for selection and menus, plus a wider crop when the four-file result appears. The camera pushes into and recenters the actual new PNG thumbnail, then returns to the folder view. The Photos window begins at a hard cut after the real double-click. No frame shows the Explorer output tile at the same time as the Photos image.

## Motion

The PNG zoom uses the real Explorer thumbnail. The camera scales into and recenters the thumbnail after it appears, then returns to the full folder view. After the real double-click, the Photos recording eases from 1.045x to 1x over 16 frames and holds. No file, control, or cursor is added or replaced.
