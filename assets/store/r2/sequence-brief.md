# Softstage Store trailer R2

## Goal

Show a real Windows user selecting three images, choosing the Softstage Explorer command, seeing the PNG output, and opening it.

## Direction

- Use the current Softstage icon, wordmark, charcoal, lilac, and Google Sans Flex.
- Use real Windows screen recordings for every product action.
- Keep the cursor visible when the capture shows it. Do not draw a cursor or recreate Windows controls.
- Crop private navigation and file paths from the Explorer recordings.
- Use short captions outside the captured Windows content.
- Speed up pauses. Keep the selection, menu, command, output, and open actions in order.
- Do not claim Store approval, signing, or purchase availability.
- Include a quiet sound mix and a matching silent version.

## Runtime

The composition runs at 30 fps and 1920 x 1080. The current edit is 933 frames, 31.1 seconds. It uses action footage from two Explorer takes and a separate Photos window capture. It is an edited sequence, not one uncut take.

The opening wordmark holds for 30 frames after its entrance. The closing holds for 60 frames. Explorer footage shows three source images and exactly one output PNG tile. The output preview pane is cropped out. The Photos shot starts after the real double-click and shows the composition in one full-window image. No frame shows two copies of the generated composition or a Photos filmstrip.

## Sound

Use the existing Mixkit transition sound at a low level. Do not add voice or music. The source sound file is for video use and must not be redistributed as a standalone audio file.
