## Capture limits

The native capture pipe disconnected before a fresh continuous result-state recording could be made. The edit therefore uses the verified 14 October Explorer stills and the matching 12 October composition output. It does not present the scenes as one take. The 16 October menu footage is genuine but is not used because it does not clearly show the pointer on the command.

The unused 16 October output and capture-worker probe files stay in `assets/store/r1/raw/`. They are not part of the trailer or Store exports.
